With twonly, you share photos and videos only with your friends. Photos, videos, and messages are end-to-end encrypted, so only you and your friends can read or view them. There are no ads. We don't track you or sell your data.

- Edit photos and videos with text, drawings, and stickers
- Set a timer to control how long sent photos stay visible
- Send photos directly to your friends' home screens
- Send text and voice messages in one-on-one and group chats
- Share stories for 24 hours
- Create your own stickers from photos
- Play mini-games together right in the chat
- Save photos and videos in Memories and back them up in the cloud with end-to-end encryption

You don't need a phone number or email address to sign up.

twonly is open source and hosted in Europe. The app is funded through an optional subscription.
